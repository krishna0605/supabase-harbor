# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and differing levels of experience.
- Respect privacy and never share credentials or private user data.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and correct harmful behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation,
doxing, credential disclosure, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when conduct violates this policy.

For sensitive conduct reports, contact the maintainers through a private GitHub
Security Advisory rather than a public issue. Maintainers will protect reporter
privacy to the extent reasonably possible.
